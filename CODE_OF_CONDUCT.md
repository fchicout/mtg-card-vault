# Code of conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge
We want participation to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour
- Be respectful, patient and constructive; assume good intent.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person.
- Keep discussion on topic and in public project spaces (issues, pull requests).

## Unacceptable behaviour
Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, publishing others' private information, and any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement
The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit or reject comments, commits and issues, or ban contributors who violate them. Report a concern privately by contacting the maintainer through GitHub (@fchicout) or, for anything sensitive, through a private security advisory. All reports are handled confidentially.
